Four parts, seven skills

SKILLS 1–2

What I hear

Active Listening and Purposeful Small Talk. Capture what is said and uncover what is not.

SKILLS 3–5

How I respond

Positive Language, Advocacy and Alternative Positioning. Frame solutions in the best possible light.

SKILL 6

Develop self-awareness

Understanding My Dominant Style. Know your strengths and your stress response.

SKILL 7

Maintain self-awareness

Flexing to Others' Personalities. Adapt so the skills hold up under pressure.

One standard for agents and leaders

Agents use the seven skills with customers. Leaders use the same seven skills with their agents, because agents tend to treat customers the way they are treated. Certification is internal and practical: theory, role plays and observed live interactions, aligned with the COPC CX Standard R8.0 (2026).

  • Effort predicts disloyalty. 96% of customers with a high-effort experience become more disloyal, versus 9% after a low-effort one.
  • Humans get the hardest contacts. Only 14% of service issues are fully solved in self-service, so the human conversation is where loyalty is won or lost.
  • Leaders design half the effort. The organization, not the agent, causes 49% of repeat calls: policy, tools and knowledge are leadership levers.
  • Measurable, not motivational. Scorecards track CES, FCR, repeat contacts, transfers and critical errors before and after.

Sources: CEB/Gartner Customer Loyalty Survey, The Effortless Experience (2013); Gartner (Aug 2024). Full source list on the portfolio.
