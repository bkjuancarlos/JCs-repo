Effort, not courtesy: engineering the customer experience

Most disloyalty comes from effort, not rudeness. A practical model of seven trainable skills that lower effort for customers and for the agents who serve them.

  • Why high-effort experiences drive disloyalty, and how to measure effort
  • The seven skills, from active listening to flexing to other styles
  • How leaders coach with the same skills they ask agents to use

Hold your AI to the same standard as your people

AI agents now answer customers, yet many get less oversight than a new hire. Seven disciplines from the COPC CX Standard, Release 8.0, applied to bots and AI agents.

  • Where AI gets less rigor than people: metrics, quality, escalation, coaching
  • A live readiness self-check the audience can take in three minutes
  • What I learned running my own AI agent on this site

Agents treat customers the way they are treated

Customer experience starts with employee experience. How competencies, career paths and evidence-based reviews turn values into everyday behavior.

Bio and headshot

Juan Carlos Munguia is a customer experience and operations leader with 15 years running service operations in fintech, mobility and large-scale BPO, including roles at Microsoft, DiDi and Teleperformance. A Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt, PMP and licensed organizational psychologist, he created the Engineering the Customer Experience framework, taught to more than 400 service and sales representatives.
